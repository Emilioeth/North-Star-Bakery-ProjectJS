Our Story

North Star Bakery started with a family who enjoyed baking for friends and neighbors. We opened our shop to share those recipes with the rest of the neighborhood.

Today, we still make our bread and treats by hand. Whether you stop in on your way to work or pick up a cake for a family birthday, we are glad to be part of your day.

Our Ingredients

We believe good baking starts with simple ingredients. We use flour, butter, eggs, and seasonal fruit, and we buy from nearby suppliers when we can.

We bake in small batches to keep our selection fresh. If you have a question about ingredients or allergies, please ask us before placing an order.
