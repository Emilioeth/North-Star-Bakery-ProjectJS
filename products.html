From a loaf for dinner to a cake for a birthday, North Star Bakery has something to share. Here are some of the things we bake.

Breads

Our breads are baked fresh and sold by the loaf. Prices range from $5 to $9.

  • Signature Loaf: A crusty loaf with a soft center, great with soup or toasted with butter.
  • Whole Wheat Bread: A hearty everyday bread for sandwiches and toast.
  • Baguette: A long, crisp loaf to serve alongside a meal.

Pastries and Cookies

Pick up a treat for breakfast or an afternoon break. Prices range from $2 to $5 each.

  • Butter Croissant: A flaky pastry with a buttery flavor.
  • Cinnamon Roll: Soft dough rolled with cinnamon sugar and topped with icing.
  • Chocolate Chip Cookie: A classic cookie filled with chocolate chips.

Cakes

Celebrate birthdays, family gatherings, and other special occasions with a cake. Prices range from $25 to $60, depending on size and decorations.

  • Vanilla Cake: Vanilla layers with buttercream frosting.
  • Chocolate Cake: Chocolate layers with chocolate frosting.
  • Carrot Cake: A spiced carrot cake with cream cheese frosting.
